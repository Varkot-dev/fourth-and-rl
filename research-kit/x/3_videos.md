# Videos

One per line: link or title, then optionally what the video argues.

Added 2026-11-17 by the research session from a YouTube search for referee analyses. The owner mentioned a referee video and repo without giving a link, so which of these he meant is not confirmed. Notes on their methods are in `docs/research/inbox/referee_and_coach_prior_work.md`. The transcripts of nine of these were later read in full; notes are in `docs/research/inbox/video_transcript_notes.md`. The videos themselves were not watched, so on-screen tables and charts are not covered.

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LH3Ck59I8V0 "We looked at 92,255 flags - NFL refs are biased" (Michael MacKelvie and ThatsGoodSports, 2026-11-04, about 1.58M views). Prices every penalty by win probability with and without the flag across 7,366 games and asks whether penalties even out; chapters cover objective versus subjective penalties, timing, playoffs, referee bias and "penalty luck" team rankings.
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KEoN3-5VrCQ "I Checked 87,000 NFL Flags to Find the Worst Refs" (BlitzTalk, about three weeks before 2026-11-17, about 128K views). A model the author calls Z.E.B.R.A. run over every regular-season and playoff game from 2000 through Week 6 of 2026, with each flag matched to the seven-man crew.
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OX6tCqzOlaY "I Found The Teams Helped Most By The Refs" (BlitzTalk, about two weeks before 2026-11-17, about 44K views). The follow-up: which teams the same model says were helped.
- Warren Sharp's YouTube playlist "NFL Referee Trends & Betting Impact" (42 videos; link not captured). Per-crew penalty rates and home and road splits, framed for betting.
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pPXZlK9puqw "Passing is better…so, why run?" (Michael MacKelvie, about a year before 2026-11-17, about 1.1M views). Burning question 2 as a popular explainer; chapters: Why Run?, Game States, Running Proof, Run vs. Pass Analysis. The description lists no methods or sources. It opens by comparing two options by yards per play.
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8By4b6cTVFU "Why Teams Come Back - The Rubber Band Effect" (Michael MacKelvie, 2025-11-26, about 734K views). Whether trailing teams really play better late and whether that is "prevent defense"; chapters include "Prevent" Analysis, Possession Math and "Just Don't Get Beat Deep". Credits Lau Sze Yui (adjusted EPA per play "rubber band" graph and a safety-depth graph), Sam Schwartzstein, Ben Baldwin and Udit Ranasaria. Bears on the late-lead and blown-lead questions.
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d60F61zVOJY "The NFL's Fourth-Down Revolution" (NFL Films, about a month before 2026-11-17, about 257K views).
- "Ep82 Should NFL Teams Be More Aggressive on Fourth Down? With David Romer" (Stanford Graduate School of Business, about ten days before 2026-11-17, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oaK-LTlADEY). Romer wrote the 2006 paper the plan cites.
- "I Checked 6,340 NFL 4th Downs to Find the Most Cautious Coach" (Flare Shorts, about two weeks before 2026-11-17, 859 views, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FGRj-n9YSXE). Uses FTN's published fourth-down data for 2024 and 2025; one chapter is titled "Aggressive isn't the same as right".
- "I Built a Model to Find the NFL's Most Overrated Coach" (Flare Shorts, 2026-11-03, 471 views, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ch3pd2E62io; its description discloses an AI-generated voiceover). A model called BENCH that tests coaches against the betting market; chapters include "Shrinking the Noise", "Does It Repeat?" and "The Chance Test".
- Other Michael MacKelvie titles that touch the project (channel https://www.youtube.com/@MichaelMacKelvie, 511K subscribers, 100 videos; descriptions not read): "The Best Team is Not the Best Team" (466K), "This is 97% of winning in football - nobody knows what it is" (223K), "Does Defense Win Championships...?" (427K), "Why We Still Don't Really Know Who's Good at Football - Entanglement Analysis" (1M).
