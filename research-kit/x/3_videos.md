# Videos

One per line: link or title, then optionally what the video argues.

Added 2026-11-17 by the research session from a YouTube search for referee analyses. The owner mentioned a referee video and repo without giving a link, so which of these he meant is not confirmed. Notes on their methods are in `docs/research/inbox/referee_and_coach_prior_work.md`. Only the descriptions and one linked methods page were read; the videos were not watched and the transcripts were not read.

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LH3Ck59I8V0 "We looked at 92,255 flags - NFL refs are biased" (Michael MacKelvie and ThatsGoodSports, 2026-11-04, about 1.58M views). Prices every penalty by win probability with and without the flag across 7,366 games and asks whether penalties even out; chapters cover objective versus subjective penalties, timing, playoffs, referee bias and "penalty luck" team rankings.
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KEoN3-5VrCQ "I Checked 87,000 NFL Flags to Find the Worst Refs" (BlitzTalk, about three weeks before 2026-11-17, about 128K views). A model the author calls Z.E.B.R.A. run over every regular-season and playoff game from 2000 through Week 6 of 2026, with each flag matched to the seven-man crew.
- "I Found The Teams Helped Most By The Refs" (BlitzTalk, about two weeks before 2026-11-17, about 44K views; link not captured). The follow-up: which teams the same model says were helped.
- Warren Sharp's YouTube playlist "NFL Referee Trends & Betting Impact" (42 videos; link not captured). Per-crew penalty rates and home and road splits, framed for betting.
